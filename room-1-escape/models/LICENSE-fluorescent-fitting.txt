fluorescent-fitting.glb and ../textures/fluorescent-fitting-color.jpg, -normal.jpg and -arm.jpg are the model
"Mounted Fluorescent Lights" by Ulan Cabanilla from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/mounted_fluorescent_lights),
fetched through the Poly Haven API (api.polyhaven.com). Only the housing of the single-tube batten ("a") is kept,
packed into one binary glTF file; its tube is left out, because room.js builds a clear glass tube with its electrodes
in its place. The ARM picture was saved again as a JPEG at quality 88.
