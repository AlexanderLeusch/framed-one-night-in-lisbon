Inmate 4471, the player (inmate.glb), is built from the MakeHuman base mesh "hm08", its body targets, its default
skeleton with its weights, and its high-poly eyes (the eyes use hale-eye.jpg, MakeHuman's brown eye texture, made
smaller). These MakeHuman assets were explicitly released as CC0 1.0 in September 2020 by their copyright holders (Data
Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier); see http://www.makehumancommunity.org and
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman. They were taken once from the makehuman 1.3.2 package and its
GitHub repository and turned into this file offline: the body shaped as a man in his late thirties, the face and hand
bones folded into their parents (66 bones), the striped prison uniform, its collar, the number patch and the boots
fitted to the body, and the skin painted (inmate-skin.jpg and inmate-skin-bump.jpg are our own work). Nothing from
MakeHuman is installed or loaded at run time.
