fire-extinguisher.glb and ../textures/fire-extinguisher-color.jpg, -normal.jpg and -arm.jpg are the model
"Korean Fire Extinguisher 01" by Um Joorin from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/korean_fire_extinguisher_01),
fetched through the Poly Haven API (api.polyhaven.com). Only the body and the gauge glass are kept (the paper tag is
left off), packed into one binary glTF file; the Korean word on the stand's label (소화기, "fire extinguisher") was
painted over and replaced by FIRE EXTINGUISHER in the colour and normal pictures.
